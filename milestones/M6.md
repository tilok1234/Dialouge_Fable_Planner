## Scope

The Stage-4 reviewer — the last piece of the generation pipeline before human
approval. Two tiers (ARCHITECTURE §6):

1. **Deterministic dialogue checks** (code, in `@df/validators`): the M4
   forbidden-facts gate becomes a standalone reviewer, plus required-facts-
   present, line-length, locked-text-unchanged, duplicate line ids, and every
   choice has a resulting state.
2. **AI-assisted checks** (provider): `MockProvider.reviewDialogue` returns
   voice/repetition/exposition findings (canned but realistic); `repairDialogue`
   fixes a flagged line deterministically.

Both report-don't-rewrite at the reviewer level; the repair stage fixes only
the flagged line, preserving locks (constraint #7). Still mock-only (Q-A1).
