## Scope

The schemas, sample, storage, and quest/scene editors already exist (Phase 0 +
M1 + M2 follow-up). What M5 adds is the **runtime machinery**: a pure validation
+ simulation layer that proves a quest plays through without early revelations
or state contradictions — the M5 acceptance bar.

This lands in `@df/validators` (the deterministic-checks package, last touched
in M0 as an empty scaffold). Three concerns:

1. **Quest structural validation**: every `transitionsTo` points at an existing
   stage; every choice's `resultingStage` exists; stage order is monotonic; no
   orphan stages (unreachable from stage 0).
2. **Knowledge-progression check**: no scene bound to stage N references (in its
   requiredFacts/hintableFacts) a canon fact that the quest only reveals at a
   stage > N. The origin must not be knowable before the quest permits it.
3. **Playthrough simulator**: walk every branch (every choice), collecting the
   facts revealed at each stage; assert no contradiction (a fact revealed at
   stage 2 isn't re-hidden at stage 3) and that every terminal stage either
   completes or fails explicitly.

Still mock-only (Q-A1 — no key, no network).

### Bugs the validators caught (the point of running them)
1. **Real sample data bug**: the foreman-offer scene (bound to stage 1) required
   `fact_seals_prevent_excavation` + `fact_foreman_ordered_last_evacuation`, but
   the quest only revealed them at stage 2. Fixed by adding both to stage 1's
   `factsRevealedToPlayer` (the foreman tells the player about the seals and the
   evacuation when offering the job).
2. **Too-strict check**: the duplicate-stage-order flag fired on the intentional
   5a/5b parallel branches. Relaxed to allow same-order stages when they're not
   reachable from each other (true branch alternatives).

## Human gate (the design doc's M5 bar)

> *A complete five-stage quest contains no early revelations or state
> contradictions.* You play the sample quest both branches and confirm nothing
> is leaked early and no state contradicts another.

The validators automate this; the human gate confirms the report is readable
and the planted-defect demonstration is convincing.
